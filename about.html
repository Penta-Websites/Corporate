Corporate Heritage & Leadership

From a modest precision toolshop in Detroit to a global multi-facility contract manufacturing powerhouse trusted by the world's most exacting innovators.

The ApexForge Standard

Built On The Science of Micron-Level Accuracy

Established in 1988, ApexForge Industrial Corp. was founded on a singular principle: that uncompromising precision and repeatable quality are the bedrocks of human technological progress.

Over three and a half decades, we have continuously reinvested 18% of annual capital expenditures directly into cutting-edge 5-axis CNC machining centers, automated robotic welding gantry systems, and Zeiss metrology suites. Today, our 120,000 m² combined plant footprint delivers mission-critical parts for orbital spacecraft, next-generation electric vehicles, deep-sea subsea manifolds, and life-saving robotic surgical tools.

Our Mission

To provide OEM engineering teams with seamless, rapid, and defect-free transition from CAD concept to scaled high-volume production.

Our Vision

To pioneer zero-defect Industry 4.0 smart autonomous factories powered entirely by carbon-neutral renewable manufacturing energy.

Evolution

Key Milestones In Our History

Over 36 years of relentless engineering advancement and global operational expansion.

1988

Founding of Apex Precision Tooling

Founded in Detroit, Michigan with 6 manual toolroom lathes and 3 milling machines serving regional automotive die makers.

2001

Aerospace Expansion & AS9100 Certification

Achieved AS9100 quality accreditation and signed initial long-term agreements for commercial aircraft jet engine mounts and brackets.

2012

European Technology Hub in Stuttgart

Opened our 35,000 m² high-speed multi-axis manufacturing facility in Baden-Württemberg, Germany to support European premium automotive and clean-energy OEMs.

2020

Robotic Cell Modernization & DMLS Additive Lab

Invested $45M in automated Industry 4.0 palletized robotic cells and industrial metal 3D printing suites for aerospace superalloys.

2026

Global Digital RFQ & Carbon-Neutral Net-Zero Milestone

Launched digital automated DFM quoting platform and achieved 84% solar self-generation across global production complexes.
