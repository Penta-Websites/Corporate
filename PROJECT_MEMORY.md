## Architecture & Technology Stack
- **Framework**: Semantic HTML5, Modular CSS3 with Custom Properties (CSS variables), Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
- **Key Modules**:
  - `css/main.css` — Global styling, typography (Barlow, Plus Jakarta Sans, JetBrains Mono), colors, layout grid, CSS custom properties.
  - `css/components.css` — Corporate header, sticky navigation, dropdowns, hero carousel, telemetry bar, cards, calculators, modal dialogs, toast notifications.
  - `css/responsive.css` — Mobile-first breakpoints, off-canvas navigation, touch ergonomics.
  - `js/main.js` — Navbar scroll state, mobile menu drawer, animated counters, industry tab switchers, search overlay modal, newsletter handling.
  - `js/rfq-calculator.js` — Interactive instant manufacturing quote engine, dynamic volume discounting, material machinability multipliers, CAD drag & drop simulation.
  - `js/machine-monitor.js` — Live mock plant capacity & telemetry simulator (OEE gauges, active cycles, spindle RPM).
- **Complete Page Directory (8 Core Pages)**:
  1. `index.html` — Corporate Homepage with Hero, KPIs, Interactive Estimator, Capabilities, Facilities, Case Studies, Testimonials, News, RFQ Banner.
  2. `about.html` — Corporate Profile, History / Heritage Timeline (1988 - Present), Leadership Team, ESG & Net-Zero Manufacturing Commitments, Global Accreditations.
  3. `capabilities.html` — Comprehensive Manufacturing Capabilities & Machinery Fleet (5-Axis Mazak, DMG Mori, Fiber Lasers, Automated CMM Zeiss Metrology, Robotic Cell specs).
  4. `industries.html` — Industry Solutions (Aerospace, Automotive & E-Mobility, Energy & Turbines, Medical & Precision Robotics, Defense & Marine).
  5. `facilities.html` — Global Smart Factories, Cleanrooms, Capacity Dashboards, Virtual Plant Tour walkthrough.
  6. `quality.html` — Metrology, CMM Inspection, Quality Assurance, ISO/AS/IATF Certifications, Failure Analysis & Traceability protocols.
  7. `case-studies.html` — Detailed Industrial Case Studies with challenges, engineering solutions, cycle time reductions, and metric badges.
  8. `rfq-quote.html` — Advanced Interactive RFQ & CAD File Upload Engine with dynamic price calculator, material selector, spec tolerances, and instant quotation generator.
  9. `contact.html` — Global Plant Locations, Plant Tour Booking Form, Emergency Machining Line, Interactive Map visualizer.
