Monolithic Titanium Impeller for Commercial Turbopump

Challenge: The client originally fabricated this high-speed cryogenic rotor by electron-beam welding four forged titanium segments together, resulting in recurring weld heat-affected-zone micro-fissures at 32,000 RPM.

Solution: ApexForge re-engineered the process for continuous 5-axis simultaneous trochoidal milling directly out of a single billet of annealed Titanium Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V). Using custom long-reach micro-endmills and dynamic CAM dampening, blade runout was held under 3 µm.

Material:
Titanium Gr.5
Tolerance:
±0.003 mm
Batch Volume:
240 Units / Yr
Electric Vehicles 18% Weight Reduction

Structural Aluminum EV Battery Pack Enclosure

Challenge: Electric delivery van manufacturer required a 2,200 × 1,400 mm battery box capable of absorbing 100G crash side impact while maintaining an IP69K hermetic seal for coolant loops.

Solution: ApexForge implemented hollow multi-chamber 6061-T6 extruded cross-members joined via automated robotic Friction Stir Welding (FSW), eliminating distortion associated with MIG welding and passing 100% helium leak tests.

Material:
Al 6063-T6 / 6061
Joining Tech:
Robotic FSW
Annual Production:
120,000 Units
Surgical Robotics Zero Defect Rate

7-DoF Articulated Micro-Wrist for Laparoscopy

Challenge: Production of ultra-miniature cable pulleys (Ø 3.2 mm) with 0.15 mm diameter wire guide grooves and mirror internal finish to prevent cable fatigue during delicate robotic surgery.

Solution: Turned on our Singapore Citizen Swiss 7-axis micro lathes with custom polycrystalline diamond (PCD) tools, achieving an Ra of 0.2 µm followed by automated ultrasonic cleanroom passivation.

Material:
Titanium 6Al-4V ELI
Surface Roughness:
Ra 0.2 µm

50,000 Units
